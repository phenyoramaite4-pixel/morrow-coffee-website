About Morrow

A neighbourhood café with a roastery out back.

Morrow started with one small roaster and a simple idea: coffee should taste like where it came from.

Our Story

Small batches, big care.

We buy directly from farms we trust, roast in small batches and brew every cup to order. Our bakers start before sunrise so the croissants are warm when the doors open.

Whether you’re grabbing a quick espresso before work or settling in with a book, there’s a table with your name on it.

What We Stand For

Three things we don’t compromise on.

Sourced with care

We work with farms we visit and pay fairly, so the people behind the beans are looked after.

Roasted in small batches

We roast weekly in our own roastery, so what reaches your cup is always fresh.

Baked by hand

Pastries and breads are made on site every morning with simple, honest ingredients.

Come and say hello.

The kettle is always on and the first cup is a good place to start.

Ways We Brew

Pick the cup that suits you.

Espresso

Rich, concentrated and the base for our milk drinks. Best for a quick, strong start.

Pour-over

Brewed by hand, one cup at a time. Best for tasting the character of a single origin.

Cold brew

Steeped slowly for a smooth, low-acid finish. Best for warm afternoons.
